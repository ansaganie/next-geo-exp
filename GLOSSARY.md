# GeoExploration

Comprehensive engineering-geological surveys, geodetic surveying, hydrogeology, water well drilling, and field exploration supplies in Kazakhstan.

## Service Categories

A **Service Category** is a broad line of work. Each **Service** (a specific offering shown as its own card, e.g. Topographic Survey, Staking Out, 3D Scanning) belongs to exactly one Service Category. Field Supplies is also a Service Category.
_Avoid_: Using "service" for the broad area

**Engineering-Geological Survey**:
Field drilling, soil sampling, laboratory analysis, and normative technical reports for construction and industrial design.
_Avoid_: Soil study, ground check

**Geodesy and Topography**:
Land surveying, topographic mapping, electronic tacheometry, and volumetric calculations for earthworks.
_Avoid_: Land measurement, cartography

**Borehole Drilling**:
Mechanical drilling operations encompassing domestic and industrial water well installation, mineral core extraction, and geotechnical site boreholes.
_Avoid_: Hole digging, drill service

**Hydrogeology**:
Assessment of groundwater reserves, aquifer dynamics, and hydrological regime monitoring.
_Avoid_: Water study

## Drilling Services

**Core Drilling** (*ru: Колонковое бурение* / *kk: Бағаналы бұрғылау*):
Rotary exploration drilling using hollow bits to recover intact cylindrical rock cores (PQ/HQ/NQ) for stratigraphic and mineral evaluation.
_Avoid_: Rock punching, tube drilling, rock core puncture

**Water Well Drilling** (*ru: Бурение скважин на воду* / *kk: Су ұңғымаларын бұрғылау*):
Drilling, filter installation, casing, and pump-testing of domestic and industrial water supply wells.
_Avoid_: Water digging, borehole punching, water hole digging

**Geotechnical Drilling** (*ru: Инженерно-геологическое бурение* / *kk: Инженерлік-геологиялық бұрғылау*):
Borehole drilling dedicated to structural foundation investigations, standard penetration testing (SPT), and undisturbed soil sampling (monoliths).
_Avoid_: Soil piercing, ground probe, engineering-geological drilling (in English)

## Field Supplies

**Core Box**:
Rigid polypropylene storage tray designed to preserve and catalog cylindrical rock core samples in PQ, HQ, and NQ dimensions.
_Avoid_: Drill case, core tray

**Sample Bag**:
Reinforced fabric container custom-manufactured for collecting, tagging, and transporting soil, crushed rock, and mineral specimens from the field to laboratories.
_Avoid_: Soil pouch, test sack
